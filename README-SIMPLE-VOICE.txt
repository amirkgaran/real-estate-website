MYINVEST — SIMPLE VOICE ASSISTANT

THIS REPLACES THE BIG VOICE MENU.

NEW EXPERIENCE
1. Visitor sees only one small round microphone button.
2. Tap it once.
3. It immediately starts listening.
4. A small bubble says "Listening..."
5. Visitor speaks the command.
6. The site performs the action.

Examples:
- "Go to Insights"
- "Read the Buying article"
- "What webinars are coming up?"
- "Register for the next webinar"
- "Show homes for sale"
- "Read listings"
- "Request details"
- "Pause"
- "Resume"
- "Stop"

WEBINAR / INQUIRY SAFETY
The assistant asks for the information by voice.
At the end, a small Confirm / Cancel card appears.
Nothing is submitted until Confirm is pressed or the visitor says the confirmation command.

IF MICROPHONE IS NOT AVAILABLE
Only then does a small typed-command box appear.
